# Doomandgloom

Forensic research project mapping the overlapping media, commercial, conference, subscription, financial-forecasting, precious-metals, preparedness, health/technology, and "collapse/control" ecosystem connected to Catherine Austin Fitts / Solari and Martin Armstrong / Armstrong Economics.

## Publication threshold

**No person or organization gets a finished profile until its ledger contains at least 50 distinct sources.**

The number alone is not enough. A 50-source profile must contain a useful mix of:
- primary / official records;
- the subject's own original statements and marketing;
- independent reporting and expert analysis;
- archived pages / dated predictions;
- customer complaints, Reddit, forums, and reviews (clearly labeled as anecdotal);
- contrary or exculpatory evidence where it exists.

A copied article, syndication, mirror, or repost is **one source**, not multiple sources.

## Core questions

1. Who is connected to whom?
2. Which connections are merely interviews, and which involve money, employment, contracts, referrals, sponsorships, affiliates, shared companies, or joint products?
3. What does each person actually sell?
4. What extraordinary claims do they make?
5. What evidence do they cite?
6. Does that evidence support the claim they make from it?
7. What dated predictions succeeded or failed?
8. What criminal, civil, regulatory, professional, or consumer history exists?
9. What complaints recur independently?
10. How does fear / collapse / secrecy / exclusivity function in the marketing model?

## Evidence labels

Every material claim in this repo should ultimately be tagged:

- **VERIFIED**
- **SUPPORTED INFERENCE**
- **DISPUTED**
- **UNVERIFIED**
- **CONTRADICTED**
- **OPINION / CUSTOMER REPORT**

The project is adversarial research, but it is not allowed to manufacture conclusions. Strong evidence should carry the argument.
