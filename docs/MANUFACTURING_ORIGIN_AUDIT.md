# Manufacturing / Origin / Supply-Chain Audit

## Core rule

Marketing language such as **"Made in USA"**, **"American made"**, **"designed in USA"**, or **"assembled in USA"** must be tested against the actual supply chain.

These phrases are not interchangeable.

For every physical product, identify where possible:
- brand owner;
- legal seller;
- contract manufacturer;
- factory;
- final assembler;
- PCB manufacturer;
- chips / sensors / modules;
- enclosure;
- battery / power supply;
- cables;
- raw materials / active ingredients;
- packaging;
- country of origin for major components;
- importer of record;
- distributor.

## Evidence sources

Use:
- product teardown photos;
- FCC filings and internal photos;
- UL / ETL / CE / other test reports;
- FDA device/manufacturer records where relevant;
- customs/import databases where lawfully available;
- bills of lading / importer records;
- patents and design filings;
- supplier catalogs;
- BOMs;
- manufacturer datasheets;
- PCB markings / chip markings;
- corporate filings;
- official warranty/returns addresses;
- marketplace product identifiers;
- archived marketing claims.

A patent proves neither manufacturing origin nor efficacy.

## Origin claim assessment

Classify:
- SUPPORTED
- PARTIALLY SUPPORTED
- ASSEMBLED IN USA / FOREIGN COMPONENTS
- DESIGNED IN USA / FOREIGN MANUFACTURE
- MISLEADINGLY WORDED
- CONTRADICTED
- INSUFFICIENT DATA

Do not treat foreign components as inherently inferior. The question is whether the marketing accurately represents origin and quality.

## Connected-manufacturer test

Specifically investigate whether any person/company in the network:
- manufactures a promoted product;
- owns the manufacturer;
- imports components;
- white-labels an existing product;
- sells the same OEM product under another brand;
- licenses the design;
- receives reseller/affiliate revenue;
- is a conference vendor or sponsor.

If one network member manufactures and another presents the item as an independent recommendation, flag the relationship for disclosure analysis.
