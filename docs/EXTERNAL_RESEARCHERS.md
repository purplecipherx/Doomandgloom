# Outside Researchers, Critics, Fact-Checkers, and Evidence Sources

## Why this track exists

We do not want Doomandgloom to become an isolated anti-network bubble.

For every major person/entity, actively find:
- people who have already investigated them;
- critics who preserve old predictions or customer experiences;
- fact-checkers who audited individual claims;
- researchers who mapped adjacent misinformation/marketing networks;
- regulators/courts holding primary records;
- the subject's rebuttals to those critics.

**Critics must be audited too.**

A negative article is not automatically reliable because it agrees with our hypothesis.

### Forums

Use these to identify **patterns and leads**, then corroborate them.

## Outsider-source scoring

- **PRIMARY** — court/regulator/filing/original document
- **A** — strong professional investigative/fact-checking source
- **B** — useful specialist/independent researcher with transparent sourcing
- **C** — advocacy/aggregator/source with meaningful limitations
- **D** — forum/review/anonymous/poorly verified material

A source's tier is not a permanent verdict. A D-tier forum post can point to an A-tier court filing.

## Research rule

Every major negative claim should ideally end with:
1. original statement;
2. independent critic/researcher;
3. primary record;
4. subject's rebuttal;
5. our own assessment.

That prevents us from simply copying somebody else's exposé.
