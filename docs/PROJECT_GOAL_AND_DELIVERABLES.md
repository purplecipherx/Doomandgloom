# Doomandgloom — Goal and Deliverables

## Direct goal

Build a reproducible, evidence-backed investigation of the wider **doom / collapse / control / alternative-finance / alternative-health / preparedness media ecosystem**.

The project must identify and document, where evidence supports it:

1. the people and organizations in the network;
2. who is actually connected to whom;
3. which relationships are media-only versus commercial, financial, ownership, research-funding, manufacturing, sponsorship, or securities relationships;
4. what each entity sells or promotes;
5. who owns, manufactures, imports, distributes, or profits from those products;
6. whether the products and claims survive independent validation;
7. whether fear, urgency, exclusivity, or extraordinary claims are used to drive sales;
8. failed predictions, contradicted factual claims, misleading citations, and post-hoc rewriting;
9. criminal, civil, regulatory, licensing, and consumer histories;
10. conflicts of interest, including undisclosed affiliates, sponsorships, positions, investments, and related-party transactions;
11. where money flows;
12. which people are the major media, commercial, financial, research-funding, and cross-cluster hubs.

The project does **not** pre-label every connected person as bad. It exposes the bad actors and bad practices that the evidence actually establishes.

# Fixed deliverables

## Deliverable 1 — Network map

Interactive/exportable graph with switchable layers:

- people/media
- formal relationships
- ownership/control
- products
- manufacturers/supply chain
- money flows
- sponsorships/affiliates
- research funding
- conferences/events
- audience transfer
- securities positions / market influence
- claim propagation

Every edge has source IDs and a relationship type.

## Deliverable 2 — Entity dossiers

Every fully investigated person/organization gets a plain-English dossier with **minimum 50 distinct sources**.

Each dossier includes:
- verified biography/credentials
- business interests
- products/services
- money relationships
- major claims
- prediction record
- conflicts
- legal/regulatory history
- negative reviews/complaints
- rebuttals/defenses
- confidence-rated findings
- complete supporting links

## Deliverable 3 — Product dossier library

For every material product/service:
- seller
- legal owner
- manufacturer
- supply chain / country-of-origin evidence
- promoters
- affiliate/sponsor relationships
- price history
- marketing claims
- actual deliverable
- independent validation
- regulatory status
- complaints
- comparable ordinary alternatives
- value/quality assessment
- documented beneficiaries

## Deliverable 4 — Follow-the-money report

Plain-English report and graph covering:
- ownership
- subscriptions
- report sales
- conference revenue
- sponsorship
- advertising
- affiliates/referrals
- precious-metals relationships
- product margins
- investments
- grants/donations
- nonprofit payments
- related-party transactions
- settlements/restitution/judgments
- securities/commodity positions where documented

## Deliverable 5 — Claim and prediction database

Searchable ledger containing:
- exact claim
- person/entity
- date
- source
- timestamp
- evidence cited
- what the evidence actually says
- assessment
- later correction/change
- associated product pitch
- associated financial interest

Predictions receive outcome scoring only when sufficiently specific and dated before the event.

## Deliverable 6 — Fear-to-commerce report

Documented examples of:

**threat/doom/control claim → urgency → CTA → product/service → beneficiary**

Each example shows:
- exact claim
- timestamp/date
- product
- price
- ownership/affiliate/sponsor relationship
- independent validation
- what is and is not proven

## Deliverable 7 — Conflict-of-interest register

All 50 locked conflict tests applied across the network.

Every entry classified:
- VERIFIED
- SUPPORTED
- SUSPECTED
- UNVERIFIED
- CONTRADICTED

## Deliverable 8 — External researcher / critic index

Who else has investigated each person/entity:
- professional journalists
- fact-checkers
- regulators
- independent researchers
- former customers/employees
- Reddit/forums/community investigators

Their claims are separately audited.

## Deliverable 9 — Evidence archive and audit trail

For every high-value source:
- source ID
- canonical URL
- archive URL
- retrieval date
- title/author
- original/derivative lineage
- local artifact reference where preserved
- SHA-256 hash
- linked entity/claim/product/money IDs
- provenance notes

## Deliverable 10 — Final layman's exposé

A readable final report that answers:

- Who are the important players?
- Who are the genuine hubs?
- What do they sell?
- Where does the money go?
- What claims fail?
- What products fail?
- Which conflicts are hidden?
- Which scary narratives directly lead into commercial pitches?
- Which accusations against them are well supported?
- Which accusations do **not** survive verification?

The report must show its work and link directly into the evidence archive.

# Data-gathering order

1. inventory channels/sites/entities;
2. ingest existing captions/transcripts;
3. audio-only fallback for captionless material;
4. ingest websites/newsletters/product pages;
5. collect court/regulator/corporate records;
6. collect external investigators and criticism;
7. extract entities/claims/products/sponsors;
8. build graph;
9. follow strongest money/product/conflict trails;
10. fill each core dossier to 50+ distinct sources;
11. red-team findings;
12. publish only what passes the evidence gate.
